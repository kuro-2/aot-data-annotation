How We Check Our Work

These checks run on every batch, and we tune them with you during the pilot.

  • Reviewer Agreement. The same item goes to more than one reviewer and we compare.
  • Coverage. Each batch includes the conditions your AI will meet in real use.
  • Spot Checks. We re-check a sample of accepted work before it ships.
  • Repeat Mistakes. Errors that keep coming back get traced and fixed at the cause.
  • Second Opinion. Subjective items go to another reviewer.

How a Pilot Runs

  1. Define

    We review your sample and agree the format, rules, edge cases and what counts as accepted.

  2. Staff

    Skill checks and a trial task pick the right contributors and reviewers.

  3. Set Up

    Automated checks flag bad records, QA reviews anything odd, and we export in your format.

  4. Scale

    Once you're happy, we grow the volume and feed lessons back into the guidelines.
